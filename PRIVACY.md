# IronMath privacy

IronMath is an offline barbell calculator. It does not create accounts, does not track you, and does not send your lifts or gym settings to a server.

Weights, units, gym plate counts, and other settings stay on this device (local app storage). There are no ads and no third-party analytics.

If you contact us, we only see what you choose to send.
