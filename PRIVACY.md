# IronMath privacy

IronMath is a barbell calculator. Weights, units, gym plate counts, rest-timer run state, and scratch calculator inputs stay on this device (local app storage).

If you create an optional IronMath account, we store your email, the name you enter, and the workout data you choose to sync (logged sets, lifts, gym profiles, 1000 lb Club numbers, and settings) on Berth so you can restore them on another phone. Sync uses Berth end-user auth. The app ships only Berth's publishable key. Account deletion is available in Settings and removes your Berth user and backed-up rows. Data that stayed only on the phone is not deleted by that action.

There are no ads and no third-party analytics.

If you contact us, we only see what you choose to send.
